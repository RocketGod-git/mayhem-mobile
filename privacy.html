Mayhem Mobile — Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026

Mayhem Mobile collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is sent to its developer, because the app has nowhere to send it.

What the permissions are for

  • USB — to talk to the SDR hardware you plug in. This is the whole point of the app.
  • Location — used on the device to centre the map and as the reference position that aircraft position messages are decoded against. Your location is never transmitted by the app and is not stored anywhere outside it.
  • Internet — used only to fetch map tiles. See below.

Map tiles

The map is drawn with tiles from OpenStreetMap. Requesting a tile tells that service your IP address and which part of the world you are looking at, the same as loading any map in a browser. Their handling of that is covered by the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy. Nothing else in the Android app makes network requests.

The browser version

This policy covers the Android app. The browser version of Mayhem Mobile, which runs on this same site, performs optional lookups while you use it: aircraft details from adsbdb, amateur callsign lookups from HamDB, Callook, HamQTH and QRZ, satellite orbit data from CelesTrak, map tiles as above, and its charting library from a public CDN. Each request tells that service your IP address and what you asked about. None of them receive an account, an identifier or anything about you beyond the lookup itself, and they only happen when you use the feature that needs them.

What stays on your device

Recorded IQ files, decoded packet logs, received messages and your settings are written to the app's own storage on your phone. They are never uploaded. Removing the app removes them.

Radio reception

The app decodes radio signals that are already being broadcast around you — aircraft, ships, pagers, utility meters, tyre-pressure sensors and similar. That data is shown to you and written to the local logs described above. It is not forwarded anywhere.

Children

This app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.
