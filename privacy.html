Mayhem Mobile — Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Mayhem Mobile collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is sent to its developer, because the app has nowhere to send it.

What the permissions are for

  • USB — to talk to the SDR hardware you plug in. This is the whole point of the app.
  • Location — used on the device to center the map and as the reference position that aircraft position messages are decoded against. Your location is never transmitted by the app and is not stored anywhere outside it.
  • Microphone — only while you hold push-to-talk to transmit your voice through a HackRF (HAM Radio and Satellites). The audio goes straight to the radio. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere else. Receive-only use never asks for it.
  • Internet — map tiles and the optional lookups listed below.

Network requests

The app has no server of its own. It talks to these public services, and only when you use the feature that needs them:

  • Map tiles from OpenStreetMap, when a map is on screen. The tile request reveals which part of the world you are looking at, the same as any map in a browser (OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy).
  • Aircraft details (type, registration, operator) from adsbdb, by the aircraft's broadcast address.
  • Amateur callsign lookups from HamQTH, HamDB and Callook, for the callsign you look up or log. Tapping QRZ opens qrz.com in your browser.
  • Satellite orbits from CelesTrak. Passes are worked out on the phone; your location is not sent.
  • Update checks: a copy installed from Google Play is updated by Play. A copy installed from the APK on this site asks GitHub whether a newer build exists.

Each request tells that service your IP address and what was asked for. None of them receive an account, an identifier, your location or anything about you beyond the lookup itself.

The browser version

The browser version of Mayhem Mobile, on this same site, makes the same lookups and also loads its charting library from a public CDN. Everything else above applies to it too.

What stays on your device

Recorded IQ files, decoded packet logs, received messages and your settings are written to the app's own storage on your phone. They are never uploaded. Removing the app removes them.

Radio reception

The app decodes radio signals that are already being broadcast around you — aircraft, ships, pagers, utility meters, tire-pressure sensors and similar. That data is shown to you and written to the local logs described above. It is not forwarded anywhere.

Children

This app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.
